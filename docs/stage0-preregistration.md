# Evidence-Grading Redesign — Stage 0: pre-registration

**Written 2026-09-06, BEFORE the shrinkage layer was run on any real ticker.**
Committed on its own, as the first commit of branch `stage0-evidence-grading`,
so its hash provably predates every number this stage produces.

> Infrastructure, Forecasting, Agents, Evaluation, Research, Horizon sweep).

## 1. The decision rule, quoted verbatim from the improvement plan

> "Stage 0 succeeds as a measurement fix if the panel-level diagnostics
> (mu_hat, tau2_hat) and the shift in grade counts are informative about
> whether the constraint is measurement power or genuine signal. If, after
> shrinkage and FDR control, the number of STRONG/WEAK names does not rise
> materially from the current 3, the honest conclusion is that the constraint
> is signal, not measurement — proceed to Stage 1 (new data sources), not to
> further loosening of this grading scheme."

That is the rule. It is not adjustable after the fact.

## 2. What is being measured

Each of the 84 frozen-universe tickers already has a held-out track record from
the existing per-ticker purged walk-forward (`pipeline.model.evaluate_ticker`,
`PurgedWalkForward`, 5 folds, 30-session horizon and embargo, `min_train=500`,
nested Optuna inside each training fold). That produces, per ticker, ~1,900
out-of-sample rows of `(date, y_true, y_pred)` spanning 2018-11 to 2026-07.

The live gate (`agents.critic_agent.grade_evidence`) reduces that to one pooled
Spearman rank IC plus a t-statistic built from
`n_effective = n / horizon ≈ 63.6`, and requires 2 of 3 point-estimate checks.

Stage 0 changes **only how that same track record is graded**. It does not
change the model, the target, the features, the folds, the horizon, the cost
model, or the LLM agents. The out-of-sample predictions are regenerated by
calling the existing `evaluate_ticker` unmodified (the walk-forward is seeded
and deterministic); nothing about how they are produced is touched.

## 3. The method, fixed in advance

1. **Per-ticker block bootstrap.** Moving-block bootstrap of each ticker's own
   `(y_true, y_pred)` OOS series. Block length = 30 sessions = the label
   horizon = the purge/embargo width, so a resample cannot manufacture
   independence the overlapping labels do not have. `BOOTSTRAP_N_RESAMPLES =
   2000`, seeded. Yields `hat_ic_i` (the point estimate, computed on the real
   series) and `sigma2_i` (the bootstrap sampling variance).
2. **Flat pooling, single grand mean.** Precision-weighted (fixed-effect) mean
   `mu_hat = Σ(hat_ic_i / sigma2_i) / Σ(1 / sigma2_i)`. Sector-hierarchical
   pooling is an explicit non-goal for this stage.
3. **Between-ticker variance** `tau2_hat` by the DerSimonian-Laird
   method-of-moments estimator, clipped at zero.
4. **Empirical-Bayes shrinkage** (James-Stein / Efron-Morris):
   `B_i = sigma2_i / (sigma2_i + tau2_hat)`,
   `theta_i = B_i * mu_hat + (1 - B_i) * hat_ic_i`.
5. **Posterior variance** `sigma2_i * tau2_hat / (sigma2_i + tau2_hat)
   + B_i^2 * var(mu_hat)`. The second term is Morris' correction for the fact
   that `mu_hat` is estimated rather than known. It is the difference between
   an honest answer and a catastrophic one: at `tau2_hat = 0` the uncorrected
   formula gives every ticker a posterior variance of exactly zero, hence
   infinite confidence, hence 84 STRONG grades out of nothing at all.
6. **Posterior probability of skill** `p_positive_i = Phi(theta_i /
   sqrt(post_var_i))`, Normal approximation.
7. **Multiple testing.** ONE Benjamini-Hochberg family of 84 **two-sided**
   p-values `p_two_i = 2 * min(p_positive_i, 1 - p_positive_i)`, at
   `FDR_Q = 0.10`. The sign of `theta_i` then routes a significant ticker to
   the positive or the anti-signal branch. One two-sided family rather than two
   one-sided families, because two families at q each is a laxer test wearing
   the same label.
8. **The economic bar** is `pipeline.portfolio.break_even_ic`, reused and not
   reimplemented, at the recorded panel `spread_per_ic = 0.3474` and
   `turnover = 0.80`: **0.0051** at zero impact.

## 4. The grading rule, fixed in advance

`STRONG_POSTERIOR_THRESHOLD = 0.90`, `FDR_Q = 0.10`.

| BH significant | sign(theta) | p_positive >= 0.90 | theta >= break-even | grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| yes | negative | — | — | **ANTI_SIGNAL** |
| yes | positive | yes | yes | **STRONG** |
| yes | positive | yes | no | **WEAK** |
| yes | positive | no | — | **WEAK** |
| no | — | yes | — | **WEAK** |
| no | — | no | — | **INSUFFICIENT** |

A ticker with no usable estimate (too few OOS rows, or a degenerate constant
prediction whose rank IC is undefined) is **INSUFFICIENT** with a stated
reason. It is never silently dropped from the panel and never crashes the run.

## 5. What would make this a failure rather than a success

- **A large, easy jump in STRONG counts is a red flag before it is good news.**
  The first response to one is to look for a bug — most plausibly a degenerate
  `tau2_hat = 0` collapsing the posterior variance, or a bootstrap whose blocks
  are shorter than the label overlap. Only after those are excluded may a rise
  be reported as a measurement result.
- **No threshold in section 3 or 4 may be changed after seeing the result** in
  order to produce more STRONG grades. If the temptation arises it is to be
  raised with the user, not acted on. Maximising the count of names that pass a
  skill gate is mechanically the same objective that produced this project's
  earlier retracted results (audit finding F4, universe selected on the metric
  being graded).
- **Shadow mode is absolute.** `forecast_confidence`, the `forecast_current`
  and `forecast` tables, the public API and the web frontend are untouched by
  this stage. The old gate keeps running unmodified as the baseline.

## 6. The prediction on record

Stated before the run, so that being wrong is visible: given that the live
per-ticker t-statistics span roughly -2.3 to +1.8 with a mean near -0.18 and an
sd near 0.92 — i.e. approximately the standard normal the null predicts —
`tau2_hat` is expected to come back at or very near **zero**, `mu_hat` near
**zero or slightly negative**, and the count of STRONG names to be **0**. If
that is what happens, the pre-registered conclusion is that the constraint is
**signal, not measurement**, and the correct next move is Stage 1.
